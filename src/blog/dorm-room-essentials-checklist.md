Moving into your first room is exciting right up until you're standing in Target (or scrolling Amazon at 1am) with no idea what you actually need. This checklist is the one I wish I'd had: everything that genuinely earns its place in a small room, organised by zone, plus the stuff you can safely skip.

**Quick tip before you buy anything:** check your housing portal for your mattress size (usually Twin XL in US dorms), what appliances are banned, and whether your room has a desk lamp, mirror or wardrobe already.

## 1. Sleep & bedding

Dorm mattresses are thin, plasticky and have seen things. Fixing your bed is the single biggest upgrade you can make.

- [ ] Mattress topper (Twin XL if you're in a US dorm)
- [ ] Mattress protector (waterproof, zip-up if you're squeamish)
- [ ] Two sets of sheets, so one can be in the wash
- [ ] Duvet or comforter + a cover you love
- [ ] Two pillows
- [ ] Sleep mask and earplugs (roommate insurance)

## 2. Study & desk setup

Dorm-issued desks are small and the overhead lighting is usually awful. A few cheap upgrades make studying in your room actually possible.

- [ ] Desk lamp (clip-on saves desk space)
- [ ] Surge-protected power strip with USB ports
- [ ] Laptop stand
- [ ] Desk organiser
- [ ] Seat cushion for the hard dorm chair
- [ ] Noise-cancelling headphones (if the budget stretches)

## 3. Storage & organisation

You will have more stuff than space. Accept it now, and plan to use vertical space, the back of the door and under the bed.

- [ ] Under-bed storage bags or boxes
- [ ] Over-the-door organiser
- [ ] Adhesive, no-drill hooks (buy a big pack)
- [ ] A rolling cart (bedside table, pantry and bathroom caddy in one)
- [ ] Collapsible storage cubes
- [ ] Shower caddy for shared bathrooms

## 4. Kitchen starter kit

Even if you're on a meal plan, a tiny kitchen kit saves you money and late-night vending-machine dinners.

- [ ] Kettle (check your dorm rules first)
- [ ] One mug, one bowl, one plate and cutlery per person
- [ ] Meal prep containers
- [ ] Reusable water bottle
- [ ] For apartments: two nonstick pans, a chef's knife, a chopping board and a utensil set

{% productGrid "electric-kettle", "meal-prep-containers" %}

## 5. Decor & lighting

This is where your room stops feeling like a hospital ward. Warm lighting does most of the work.

- [ ] Warm white string lights
- [ ] A rug for cold floors
- [ ] Photos and prints (photo clip lights are perfect)
- [ ] Removable wall decor or wallpaper
- [ ] A plant (real or very convincing fake)

{% productGrid "led-string-lights", "photo-clip-lights" %}

## What NOT to bring

- **Candles and wax melts.** Banned in almost every dorm. Use LED lights instead.
- **A full-size printer.** The library has one.
- **Anything that needs drilling.** You'll lose your deposit.
- **Duplicates of shared items.** Message your roommate before you both buy a mini fridge.
