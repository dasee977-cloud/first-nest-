There's a specific kind of sadness to a freshly unpacked room: white walls, one overhead light, a bare floor and an echo. The good news is that "cosy" is mostly about three things: **light, texture and personal stuff**. You can fix all three for under $100.

## The $100 cosy plan

| What | Why it matters | Approx. cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm string lights | Replaces harsh overhead light | $10–$16 |
| Sunset or accent lamp | A second warm light source | $15–$24 |
| Photo clip lights | Personal photos + more glow | $9–$14 |
| Washable rug | Warmth and texture underfoot | $35–$60 |
| Throw or extra cushion | Makes the bed feel like a sofa | $10–$15 |
| **Total** | | **~$79–$129** |

Go for the lower end of each range (or skip the second lamp) and you'll land comfortably under $100.

## Step 1: Kill the overhead light

Overhead lighting is the number one reason a room feels cold. Turn it off and layer two or three warm, low light sources instead. Fairy lights along the bed frame or around a window are the cheapest, fastest win.

{% product "led-string-lights" %}

## Step 2: Add a glow you can actually see by

String lights are mood; you'll also want a lamp. A sunset projection lamp throws a warm wash of colour across a wall, and it's basically made for photos.

{% product "sunset-lamp" %}

## Step 3: Cover the floor

Cold tile or questionable carpet makes any room feel temporary. A rug adds warmth, softens the echo and defines your space. Machine-washable is a must in shared housing.

{% product "washable-rug" %}

## Step 4: Make it yours

Personal stuff is free cosiness. Print your favourite photos (phone photo kiosks cost cents per print), postcards from home or gig tickets, and hang them on photo clip lights for a gallery wall that doubles as lighting.

{% product "photo-clip-lights" %}

## Step 5: Soften the bed

In a small room, your bed is also your sofa. Pile it with a throw, an extra cushion and a duvet cover in a warm colour so it looks inviting during the day.

{% productGrid "twin-xl-sheet-set", "all-season-duvet" %}

## Free (or almost free) cosy hacks

- **Bring something from home.** A blanket, a mug, a framed photo.
- **Add one living thing.** A cheap pothos or snake plant is nearly unkillable.
- **Use scent safely.** Candles are banned in most dorms; try a reed diffuser instead.
- **Tidy the cables.** Visual clutter makes a room feel smaller. Adhesive cable clips cost a couple of dollars.
