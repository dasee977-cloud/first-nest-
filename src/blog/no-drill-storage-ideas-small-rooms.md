Small rooms come with two problems: nowhere to put anything, and a lease (or a residence hall) that says you can't put a single hole in the wall. The good news is that you can get a surprising amount of storage out of a tiny room without picking up a drill. Here's how.

## 1. Go vertical with adhesive hooks

Damage-free adhesive hooks are the most useful thing you can own as a renter. Use them for towels, bags, headphones, keys, jackets, string lights and even small shelves. The secret is following the instructions: clean the wall with rubbing alcohol, press for 30 seconds, and wait an hour before hanging anything.

## 2. Claim the back of the door

The back of your door is prime real estate that most people ignore. An over-the-door organiser holds shoes, toiletries, snacks, cleaning supplies or accessories. No tools needed: it just hooks over the top.

{% product "over-door-organiser" %}

## 3. Use the space under your bed

If your bed is lofted or on risers, the floor underneath is basically a hidden wardrobe. Zip-up bags with clear windows are perfect for off-season clothes, spare bedding and things you don't need every day.

{% product "under-bed-storage" %}

## 4. Add a rolling cart

A three-tier rolling cart is a bedside table, a snack pantry, a bathroom caddy or an art station. Because it's on wheels, it slides into whatever awkward gap your room has, and moves with you to your next place.

{% product "rolling-cart" %}

## 5. Hide clutter in fabric cubes

Open shelving looks messy fast. Collapsible fabric cubes turn a jumble into something that looks intentional, and they fold flat when you move out.

{% product "stackable-bins" %}

## 6. Organise your desk upward

A desk organiser with drawers stops your study space turning into a pile of chargers and receipts. Choose one that's taller rather than wider to keep the desk surface free.

{% product "desk-organiser" %}

## More no-drill tricks

- **Tension rods** inside a wardrobe or alcove create an extra hanging rail or shelf.
- **Command-style picture strips** hold up lightweight shelves and frames.
- **Bed risers** add up to 6 inches of under-bed clearance in seconds.
- **Magnetic strips** stick to a mini fridge for knives, keys or notes.

## The renter's golden rule

Before you stick anything to a wall, test one strip somewhere hidden. Some painted walls (especially fresh paint) peel when adhesive comes off. And keep the removal instructions: pulling the tab straight down, slowly, is what keeps the wall clean.
